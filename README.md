# Vrinda Store Analysis

## 1. Business problem statements
Vrinda Store is an e-commerce retail business selling clothing products through multiple online platforms. Despite generating sales across different customer segments, states, product categories, and sales channels, the business needs a clearer understanding of its sales performance and customer purchasing behavior.<br>
Management wants to identify the factors influencing sales, understand which customer segments contribute the most revenue, evaluate order fulfillment performance, and determine which sales channels and product categories perform best. To address these challenges, the business requires a data-driven analysis of its 2022 sales data to uncover growth opportunities, improve marketing effectiveness, and make informed decisions about customer targeting and sales strategies.

## 2. Goal of this dashboard
The goal of this dashboard is to analyze Vrinda Store’s 2022 sales performance by comparing monthly sales and order trends, identifying key customer segments based on gender and age, evaluating state-wise sales contributions, and measuring the performance of different sales channels and product categories. It also helps stakeholders monitor order fulfillment status and understand purchasing patterns to improve customer targeting, optimize marketing campaigns, and increase overall sales.

## 3. Insights
### 1. Monthly Sales and Orders
* Which month recorded the highest sales and orders? March recorded the highest sales and order volume in the dashboard, at approximately ₹1.9 million in sales.<br>
* How did sales and orders change throughout the year? Both generally declined after March, with some recovery in August, followed by a weaker performance toward the end of the year.<br>
* What does this indicate? Vrinda Store should investigate the reasons for March's strong performance and develop campaigns to improve sales during weaker months.<br>

### 2. Customer Gender Analysis
* Who purchased more: men or women? Women accounted for approximately 64–65% of purchases, while men contributed around 35–36%.<br>
* What does this indicate? Women are the primary customer segment and should remain a major focus of customer acquisition and retention campaigns.<br>

### 3. Order Status Analysis
* What were the different order statuses in 2022? Approximately 92% of orders were delivered, 3% returned, 3% cancelled, and 2% refunded.<br>
* What does this indicate? Delivery performance is strong, but returns, cancellations, and refunds represent opportunities to improve customer experience and reduce avoidable losses.<br>

### 4. State-Wise Sales Analysis
* Which are the top five states contributing to sales? Maharashtra (₹2.99M), Karnataka (₹2.65M), Uttar Pradesh (₹2.10M), Telangana (₹1.71M), and Tamil Nadu (₹1.68M).<br>
* Which states should receive greater marketing attention? Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the leading three states, together contributing approximately 35% of sales, according to the provided insights.<br>
* What does this indicate? These markets offer opportunities for targeted promotions, customer retention, and regional campaign expansion.<br>

### 5. Age and Gender Analysis
* Which age group contributes the most? Adults aged 30–49 are the largest contributing age group, accounting for approximately 50% of purchases according to the provided insights.<br>
* How do age and gender influence purchasing? Women contribute more than men across the adult, senior, and teenage groups shown in the dashboard. Adult women are the largest individual age-and-gender segment.<br>
* What does this indicate? Marketing campaigns should prioritize adult customers, particularly women, while tailoring offers to other age groups.<br>

### 6. Sales Channel Analysis
* Which channel contributes the maximum sales? Amazon leads with 35.5% of sales, followed by Myntra at 23.4% and Flipkart at 21.6%.<br>
* How much do the top three channels contribute? Together, Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart account for approximately 80.5% of sales.<br>
* What does this indicate? These platforms are essential sales channels. Vrinda Store should strengthen its presence on them while exploring opportunities to grow other channels.<br>

### 7. Product Category Analysis
* Which is the highest-selling product category? The provided dashboard does not display a sales-by-category chart, so the leading category cannot be confirmed from this screenshot.<br>
* What should be investigated? Compare sales, order volume, and average order value across categories such as Blouse, Bottom, Ethnic Dress, and Kurta to identify the best-performing products.<br>

## 4. Recommendations
1. Target high-value customers: Prioritize women aged 30–49 with personalized offers, relevant promotions, and loyalty campaigns.<br>
2. Strengthen leading markets: Focus on Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh while testing campaigns in other states to expand reach.<br>
3. Optimize sales channels: Continue investing in Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra, while testing strategies to improve sales through Ajio, Meesho, and other channels.<br>
4. Improve weaker-month performance: Investigate the March sales peak and develop seasonal promotions, discounts, and remarketing campaigns to support demand throughout the year.<br>
5. Reduce returns and cancellations: Analyze return reasons, product descriptions, sizing issues, delivery delays, and customer complaints to identify preventable losses.<br>
6. Optimize product assortment: Build a category-level sales report to identify popular products, underperforming categories, and opportunities for cross-selling.<br>
7. Track business performance: Monitor monthly sales, order volume, delivery rate, return rate, and channel contribution to evaluate the impact of future strategies.<br>
